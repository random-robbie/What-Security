About Me

{% if site.tagline %}{{site.tagline}}{% else %}Security Researcher{% endif %}

I'm Robbie Wiggins, a UK-based security researcher known as "txt3rob" on HackerOne and Bugcrowd. I do full-time vulnerability research and responsible disclosure — finding security holes everywhere, then reporting them so they get fixed.

Some findings that made the news: a hardcoded database password in the Applebee's Android app that exposed customer data (covered by TechCrunch), a Jira bug that exposed private server keys at major companies (ZDNet), 50,000 Honda India customers left exposed on a public AWS S3 bucket (BleepingComputer), and a tip-off alert service for exposed Amazon cloud storage (BBC). My work has been covered by TechCrunch, ZDNet, the BBC, TechRadar, The Telegraph, BleepingComputer and SC Media — the full list is on the media page.

That work has earned me recognition in 17 vendor Halls of Fame, including Apple, Google, Microsoft, Dell, AT&T, AOL, CenturyLink, SAP, Auto Trader and CERT-EU. Beyond vendor programmes, I've helped fix over 5,000 vulnerabilities through Open Bug Bounty — mass scanning done carefully, with no disruption to the targeted websites, and reported purely to inform and assist, without expecting compensation.

Away from bug hunting I build side projects — a live flight tracker, a UK pint price database, a Bluesky bot posting National Rail disruptions, and more on the Lab page. The open source tools behind a lot of my research are on my projects page.
